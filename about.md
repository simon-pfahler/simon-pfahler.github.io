I am a PhD student in physics at the University of Regensburg, working under the supervision of Tilo Wettig.

My research applies machine learning to problems in theoretical physics and bioinformatics.
My main projects are gauge-equivariant neural networks for preconditioners in lattice QCD and efficient software for cancer progression models (Mutual Hazard Networks).

Previously, I studied physics at the University of Regensburg, with a semester abroad at the University of Southern Denmark.
